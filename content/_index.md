## How I Work

### We get clear first.
Before we tour homes or talk pricing, we figure out what matters most to you. Budget, lifestyle, timeline — all of it. Clarity upfront makes everything easier.

### We look at the numbers.
We review comparable sales and current conditions so you understand what a place is really worth. No hype. No guessing.

### We make calm decisions.
If it’s the right fit, we act. If it’s not, we walk away. There’s no pressure to force something that doesn’t feel right. Clients often tell me the process felt calmer than they expected. That’s intentional. This is a big move. You deserve clear advice and someone who’s looking out for you — not just trying to close a deal.
